HOW IT WORKS

From dining hall to done.

CampusBite fits the meals you're already eating. Here's the whole journey, five steps setup to daily habit.

01 · SET UP

Find your campus

Search your university and CampusBite loads the real dining-hall menus your meal plan already covers.

  • ✓ Every hall on your campus, in one place
  • ✓ Menus refresh automatically every morning
02 · PERSONALIZE

Build your plan in a minute

Answer a few quick questions about your goal, body, activity, and dietary needs, and we build your targets.

  • ✓ Targets from your goal, not a generic diet
  • ✓ See how many menu items already fit your plan
03 · EVERY DAY

Browse menus made for you

See exactly what every dining hall is serving today, filtered to fit your diet before you even walk in.

  • ✓ Off-diet items flagged before you eat them
  • ✓ Calories and protein on every dish
04 · LOG

Log a whole day in seconds

Swipe to log breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Calories and macros fill in automatically, no searching a database.

  • ✓ Swipe right to log, left to skip
  • ✓ Ate something off-plan? Log it in a tap
05 · STAY ON TRACK

Watch your progress build

Streaks, weekly trends, and gentle insights show what's working, and never shame a meal.

  • ✓ Streaks and weekly goal tracking
  • ✓ Trends and insights that keep you going

That's the whole thing.

Set up once, then it's one swipe a meal. Free on iOS and Android.
