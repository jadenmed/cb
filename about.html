ABOUT US

Campus dining, made for you

Millions of students eat most meals on a campus plan, with almost no tools to eat well while doing it.

Our mission

CampusBite makes the food that's already on campus work for students' health and goals, no extra cooking, no generic diet app that has no idea what your dining hall serves. Just your real menus, turned into a plan that fits you.

Why we built it

We were students trying to hit protein or eat around allergies with nothing but a printed menu and guesswork. Every nutrition app assumed we cooked our own food. None knew what was at the dining hall. So we built the one that does.
